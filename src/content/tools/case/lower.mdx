faq:
  - q: >-
        Is this the same as snake_case in lowercase?
    a: >-
        No. Lower case does not join words and does not insert underscores. `Hello World` becomes `hello world`. For `hello_world`, use the snake_case page.
  - q: >-
        What about Unicode?
    a: >-
        JavaScript's `toLowerCase`, which follows Unicode case mapping. `İ` in Turkish is the famous exception — this page uses the default locale, not `tr`.
  - q: >-
        Does it change non-letters?
    a: >-
        No. Digits, spaces, punctuation and emoji are left alone.

## Letters only

Lower case is a mapping on characters, not a joining of words. That is why this page exists next to camelCase and snake_case rather than as a mode of those: `HTTP_STATUS` becomes `http_status` here, and `httpStatus` on the camelCase page.

The other six conventions are listed under the result, so a paste meant for a different one does not mean opening a different tab.
