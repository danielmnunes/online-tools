## Lowercase, with underscores

The convention in Python, in many SQL schemas, and in a lot of JSON from backends that are not JavaScript. Words are split, lowercased, and joined with `_`.

Round-trip with camelCase holds for ordinary identifiers: `hello_world` → `helloWorld` → `hello_world`.
