description: >-
    Convert an identifier to CONSTANT_CASE — words in uppercase, joined with underscores. C macros, env vars, enum members.
faq:
  - q: >-
        Why not just uppercase?
    a: >-
        Because `hello world` in UPPER CASE is `HELLO WORLD`, with a space. CONSTANT_CASE is `HELLO_WORLD`. Env vars and C macros cannot have spaces.
  - q: >-
        snake_case in uppercase?
    a: >-
        Yes. Same words, same underscores, different case of the letters.
  - q: >-
        Digits?
    a: >-
        Kept as their own words when they sit on a letter boundary: `v2Draft` becomes `V_2_DRAFT`.

## Uppercase, with underscores

`HTTP_STATUS`, `MAX_LENGTH`, `DATABASE_URL`. The splitter is the same one camelCase uses, so `httpStatus` and `http-status` both become `HTTP_STATUS`.

Use UPPER CASE when you want the letters loud and the spaces kept. Use this page when the value has to be a single token.
