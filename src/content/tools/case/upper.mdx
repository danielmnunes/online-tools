faq:
  - q: >-
        How is this different from CONSTANT_CASE?
    a: >-
        UPPER CASE leaves separators alone: `hello world` becomes `HELLO WORLD`. CONSTANT_CASE splits into words and joins them with underscores: `HELLO_WORLD`.
  - q: >-
        German ß?
    a: >-
        In default Unicode mapping `ß` uppercases to `SS`. That is the standard, and it is what this page does.
  - q: >-
        Will it touch digits?
    a: >-
        No.

## A mapping, not a join

Uppercasing a sentence and turning an identifier into a C macro are different jobs. This page does the first. CONSTANT_CASE, next to it in the list, does the second.

Use this when the letters should shout and the spaces should stay.
