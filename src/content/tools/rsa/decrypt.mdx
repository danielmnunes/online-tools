description: >-
    Decrypt RSA-OAEP ciphertext with a PKCS#8 private key or a JWK. The hash has to match the one used to encrypt.
faq:
  - q: >-
        Decryption failed.
    a: >-
        The hash, the key or the ciphertext does not match. OAEP does not have a friendly padding-oracle error; it just fails. Confirm the public key that encrypted this is the pair of the private key you pasted, and that the hash is the same one.
  - q: >-
        BEGIN RSA PRIVATE KEY is rejected.
    a: >-
        That is PKCS#1. Convert it: `openssl pkcs8 -topk8 -nocrypt -in key.pem`. The browser wants PKCS#8.
references:
  - label: >-
        RFC 8017 §7.1 — RSAES-OAEP
    url: https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc8017#section-7.1
---

## Decrypting RSA-OAEP

Paste the PKCS#8 private key, the ciphertext, and the OAEP hash that was used
to encrypt. The result is the original message, at most a couple of hundred
bytes. There is no streaming, and there is no PKCS#1 v1.5 mode.
